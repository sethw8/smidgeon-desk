    How we work

    1. Each Claude reads this Desk at the start of a Smidgeon session and says what's new from the other side.
    2. Decisions are only marked made when the person has said so. Drafts say they're drafts.
    3. New asks become tasks, with who they're for. Finished work gets a log line, newest first.
    4. Longer material (copy rewrites, specs, stories) lives in its own doc, linked from the task or log.
    5. Say "check the Desk" or "log this to the Desk" and your Claude does the rest.
